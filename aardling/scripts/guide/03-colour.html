Seven brand colours, four functional

The brand colours come from the original brand guide. The functional colours were in the website's CSS without ever having been approved, and are kept because each does a job the brand colours cannot.

Brand

Night Blue#181C2F
Snow White#F6F6F6
White#FFFFFF
Violet#B9BBF4
Lime Green#C5EEB3
Ocean Blue#4B7BC3
Yellow#F8E181
Pink#F3B0B0

White is not in the original guide. It is listed because cards and panels sit on it, and a ground that carries text has to be an approved half of a pair.

Functional

Hover Green#98DE7EThe hover and focus state of the primary button.
Muted Grey#6B6B6BSecondary text: dates, captions, metadata.
Hairline Grey#D7D7D7Hairline rules, and the border of an unchecked control.
Error Red#CF1322Form error messages.

Muted Grey is set where it clears 4.5:1 on both White and Snow White; a lighter grey does not, however good it looks. The original guide states CMYK values alongside each colour, but they do not match a standard conversion from the same hex and name no profile, so they are not reproduced — get CMYK from your printer against a named profile.

Colour What is not approved

Four things the palette does not do

Ocean Blue as body text

4.27:1 on White, 3.95:1 on Snow White. It is a details colour: large display text, icons and rules. The website uses it as the link hover colour at 18px, which fails; links hover to Night Blue instead.

Ocean Blue as a background

Night Blue on it is 3.95:1 and Snow White on it is 3.95:1. There is no readable text colour for that ground, so it is never a ground.

The four pastels as text

Violet, Lime Green, Yellow and Pink are grounds and details. Night Blue is the only foreground approved on any of them.

Any pair not in the table

Including pastel on pastel, and any of the greys on a pastel.

Not every approved pair carries a mark

Nine of the sixteen pairs are also approved for the logotype and the icon logo. A pair approved for secondary text, form errors or hairline rules is approved for that, and a mark is none of those things. The nine are on page 14.

The original guide holds two colour tables that overlap and disagree. Ocean Blue is tagged Details in one and Backgrounds, Details in the other; the package resolves that as details only, on the contrast evidence. The guide also instructs "Dark Mode: use Grey for titles & body text" while defining no colour called Grey. That instruction is not carried over.
