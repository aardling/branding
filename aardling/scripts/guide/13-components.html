Components Callouts

Callouts

  1. A callout is a passage read differently from the one above it.
  2. Two shapes: a panel interrupts a column, a band spans the page.

This assumes a running system

Everything below describes work on software that is already in production. For a system that does not exist yet, start at the beginning instead.

  1. The panel is Violet, --radius-md, --space-4, no wider than its column. Night Blue on Violet: 9.21.
  2. Yellow and Pink are for section grounds and cards. See page {{FOLIO:colour}}.
ShapeSetting
PanelInline in a column. --radius-md, --space-4 padding, no elevation. An optional icon at --icon-lg, --space-1 from its label.
BandFull bleed. Violet, Yellow or Pink, or Night Blue under data-surface="dark". Vertical padding --space-6, --space-10 from 720px, --space-12 from 960px. No radius.

Pastel on a dark page

A callout carries data-surface="light", always. Without it the focus ring is Snow White, 1.69 against Violet. See page {{FOLIO:web}}.

  1. No grey inside a callout: no fine print, caption or metadata.
  2. One at a time. Set two consecutive callouts as one.
  3. Never two coloured bands touching. Put a page-ground section between them.

PartSetting
AttributionAll --font-size-small. Name --text-primary, semibold. Role and company --text-secondary, regular, joined with ", ", or company to a bare name with " · ". Each optional; each needs a name. Company drops when a logo is present.
PhotoSquare, --radius-sm, object-fit: cover, --control-height-sm (44px). Always a 1px --border-hairline, inline or in a card. Never a circle.
Company logoThe company's own mark, never recoloured, --icon-lg (24px) high, width following. Always an accessible name, alt="Meridian". For a dark ground, ask for a dark variant; without one, light theme only.
