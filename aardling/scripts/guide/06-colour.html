Colour Functional colours

Functional colours

Hover Green#98DE7EThe hover and focus state of the primary button.
Muted Grey#6B6B6BSecondary text, and the boundary of a form control.
Hairline Grey#D7D7D7Hairline rules. Never a control boundary; it does not measure.
Error Red#CF1322Form error messages.
Success Green#38761EConfirmation messages.
  1. Muted Grey clears 4.5:1 on both White and Snow White. Never use a lighter grey.
  2. Success Green is the brand green darkened to text strength: hue 102, between Lime Green and Hover Green.
  3. Success Green matches Error Red's weight: 5.56 against 5.57 on White, 5.14 against 5.16 on Snow White.

Colour Roles

Roles

  1. Components reference a role, never a palette colour. In a stylesheet, write the role.
  2. A theme re-points the roles; the palette does not move. --colour-snow-white is Snow White in every theme.
RoleLightDark
--surface-pageSnow WhiteNight Blue
--surface-raisedWhiteDark Surface
--text-primaryNight BlueSnow White
--text-secondaryMuted GreyDark Muted Grey
--text-errorError RedDark Error Red
--text-successSuccess GreenDark Success Green
--border-controlMuted GreyDark Muted Grey
--border-hairlineHairline GreyDark Hairline Grey
--focus-ringNight BlueSnow White
--link-underlineNight Blue at 50%Snow White at 50%
  1. Ten roles. An eleventh role is a request to add a colour and needs the same approval.
  2. --text-error is for what went wrong, --text-success for what passed.
  3. --link-underline is the one role that is not a palette colour: --text-primary at 50%, the link underline at rest.
  4. --link-underline is translucent, not a flattened grey, so it sits at half strength on a raised surface too.

Colour Pastels and marks

Pastels on the dark ground

  1. Violet, Lime Green, Yellow and Pink are grounds. Night Blue is the only foreground on them.
  2. That pair measures the same in both themes, 9.21 to 13.05. A pastel section and the primary button are identical in both themes.
  3. A focus ring inside a pastel section is Night Blue, not Snow White.

Which pairs carry a mark

  1. Nine of the eighteen light pairs are approved for the logotype and the icon logo. See page {{FOLIO:logos}}.
  2. A pair approved for secondary text, form errors, confirmations or hairline rules never carries a mark.
  3. No mark on Dark Surface. A mark on a dark card uses the Night Blue lockup.

Colour Not approved

Not approved

Ocean Blue as body text

  1. 4.27 on White, 3.95 on Snow White and Night Blue.
  2. Large display text, icons and rules, in either theme.
  3. A link never changes colour on hover. The underline carries the change.

Ocean Blue as a background

  1. Night Blue and Snow White both measure 3.95 on it.
  2. No text colour reads on it, so it is never a ground.

The four pastels as text

  1. Violet, Lime Green, Yellow and Pink are grounds and details. Night Blue is the only foreground on them, in either theme.
  2. Pink is not the dark error colour. Lime Green is not the success colour.

Either green on the wrong ground

  1. Success Green: 3.03 on Night Blue, 2.57 on Dark Surface.
  2. Dark Success Green: 2.67 on White, 2.47 on Snow White.
  3. Neither green is a background. A confirmation sits on the surface it is already on.

Any pair not in the tables

  1. Including pastel on pastel, and any grey on a pastel.
