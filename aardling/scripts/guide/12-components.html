A card title is never Voyage: the display face is spent once on a page, and cards arrive in threes and fours. A linked card does not move. The whole card is the hit area, hover thickens the title's underline from 1px to 3px, and focus rings the card rather than the title — there is no approved hover ground for a card, and page {{FOLIO:web}} rules out movement.

Components Callouts

Violet is the callout

A passage that has to be read differently from the one above it. Two shapes, and the difference between them is width: a panel interrupts a column, a band spans the page.

This assumes a running system

Everything below describes work on software that is already in production. For a system that does not exist yet, start at the beginning instead.

Violet, --radius-md, --space-4, and no wider than the column it interrupts. Night Blue on Violet is 9.21. Yellow and Pink are section grounds and cards; the callout is Violet, which is what page {{FOLIO:colour}} already says.

ShapeSetting
PanelInline in a column. --radius-md, --space-4 padding, no elevation. An optional icon at --icon-lg, --space-1 from its label.
BandFull bleed. Violet, Yellow or Pink, or Night Blue under data-surface="dark". Vertical padding --space-6, --space-10 from 720px, --space-12 from 960px. No radius.

A pastel on a dark page declares itself light

A callout on a dark ground carries data-surface="light", and it is not optional: the pastels are the same colour in both themes, so what sits on them has to be too. Without the attribute the focus ring inside the callout inherits Snow White and measures 1.69 against the Violet. The mechanism is on page {{FOLIO:web}}.

No grey inside a callout — so no fine print, no caption and no metadata; one that needs fine print is too long to be a callout. One at a time: two callouts in a row are a list, and should be set as one. Never two coloured bands touching: put a page-ground section between them, or neither one is emphasis any more.

The footer declares its own ground

One footer, on every page, and one attribute does the whole job: <footer data-surface="dark">. It sets all nine roles to their dark values, so the footer is the same in both themes and nothing inside it hand-sets a colour.
