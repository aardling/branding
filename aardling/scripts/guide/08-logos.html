Logos guidelines/logos.md

Logos

  1. Logotype: 623 × 131 units. Icon logo: 167 × 107.
  2. Both are transparent and filled with currentColor. Set color on the parent.
  3. The icon logo is the double-A ligature of the logotype, at 1:1.

Clear space

{{TEXT:assets/logos/aardling-logotype.svg}}
  1. Clear space on all four sides is the height of the icon logo: 106 units, for both marks.
  2. On the logotype, 106 units is 81% of the mark's height.

Lockups

  1. A lockup is mark, ground and clear space flattened into one fixed-size file.
  2. Use one only where you cannot supply a ground or set color.
  3. Lockups are generated from the two masters. Never edit one by hand.
  4. The icon canvas is square, for avatar uploads. Clear space: 106 horizontal, 136 vertical.

Ocean Blue

  1. An Ocean Blue logotype is never narrower than 141px, a 189px lockup.
  2. At 141px its cap height (106 of 131 units) reaches 24px, the palette's floor for Ocean Blue display text.
  3. Never draw the icon logo in Ocean Blue.
  4. This is the only colourway whose minimum differs from the mark's own.

Which mark

  1. Logotype: big touchpoints where the name should be read — site header, slide, social post.
  2. Icon logo: small touchpoints — favicon, avatar, thumbnail — and anywhere the logotype would be illegible.
