Gradients

Vesper

  1. Near-black to deep violet, a Pink glow low left, Ocean Blue in the far corner.
  2. Quiet dark ground: behind a slide of content, a section band that must not compete.
  3. No grain. With grain, white falls from 6.57:1 to 5.20:1.

Understory

  1. The darkest field: Ocean Blue at one corner, Lime Green at the other.
  2. Dark ground meant to be looked at: a cover, a full-bleed ground. Never behind something else that matters.
  3. No grain. With grain, white falls to 4.60:1, under the 5.07:1 floor.

Bloom

  1. Snow White into Violet, with Lime Green and Pink blooms.
  2. Light ground. The only one that takes Night Blue text-adjacent panels well.
  3. Grain, drawn as an SVG filter.

Harvest

  1. Snow White into Yellow, a second Yellow bloom low, Pink opposite, a Lime Green highlight.
  2. Warm ground: social cards, event artwork, a title slide.
  3. Grain, drawn as an SVG filter.
  1. SVG, 1600 × 1000, xMidYMid slice: crops to any box with a real aspect ratio.
  2. Each stays at one end of the luminance range. A colourway spanning the range takes no mark and does not ship.
  3. Artwork, not colour. Never sample a pixel into the palette; never recolour ad hoc.

Imagery Illustrations

Illustrations

  1. No text on an illustration: no heading, no caption.
  2. Text goes beside it, or on a solid panel next to it.
  1. Painterly, so raster. Shipped at 1600px as WebP with a PNG beside each.

Alternatives, in order

One-to-one coaching

  1. Text beside the image.
  2. Text on a solid panel adjacent to it.
  3. The image reduced to an accent.

Print: use the 5000 × 5000 masters and CMYK TIFFs in the Aardling - Brand shared Google Drive.

Favicon

  1. The icon logo in Night Blue on a rounded tile cut from gradient-bloom.svg.
  2. favicon.svg is the source; every raster is generated from it.
